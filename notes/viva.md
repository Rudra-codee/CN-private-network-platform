# Viva Question Bank — Phase 1 & System Fundamentals

This question bank contains key conceptual questions that all team members must be prepared to answer during evaluation.

## Question Bank

- What is the purpose of the DNS server?
- Why are we using `.test` instead of `.local`?
- What happens after DNS resolution?
- What is the TCP three-way handshake?
- Why does HTTPS use TCP?
- What happens during the TLS handshake?
- What is TLS termination?
- What is a reverse proxy?
- Why does the client not know the backend IP?
- What is load balancing?
- How does round-robin work?
- Why are ports important?
- Why does HTTPS traffic appear encrypted in Wireshark?
- What is the difference between DNS resolution and TCP connectivity?
- What is Cache-Control?
- What is a 304 response?
